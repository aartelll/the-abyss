Before you join us

The Players Code

Rules you have to follow as a player.

01
Respect others

Harassment, heavy trolling, and toxic speech are forbidden.

02
Play fair game

Cheating, stealing or any detrimental action isn't welcome.

03
Ask before you act

This project follows a strict design system, and a lot of people work on it. Please respect their work and ask before building or altering anything.

04
Listen to feedback

If people point out something, acknowledge the issue.

Before You Descend

The Abyss Code

Things every Dweller agrees to before their first session.

01
Follow The Schedule

We will precisely schedule Roleplay time. Everytime else its OoC.

02
No Meta-Gaming

What your character knows comes from what they've lived, not what you've read in another thread.

03
Consent First

Major harm, capture, or character death always needs an OOC yes first.

04
Permadeath Is A Choice

The Abyss can take a character forever — but only if the writer chooses that ending(Scriptwriters might try to encourage you if that would be fitting).

05
Stay In Character

When its Roleplay timewindow, stay in character whether you're participating in some pre-talked lore or not.

06
Escalate Through Story

Conflict between characters gets written out, not argued about after.
